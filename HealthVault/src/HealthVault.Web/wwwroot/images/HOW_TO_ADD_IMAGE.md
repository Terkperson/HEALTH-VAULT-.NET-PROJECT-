# How to Add Your Healthcare Image

## Steps to Add an Image

1. **Save your healthcare image** in this folder (`wwwroot/images/`)
   
2. **Rename it to**: `healthcare-hero.jpg` (or .png)

3. **The image will automatically appear** on the home page hero section

## Recommended Image Specifications

- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Dimensions**: 1920x600 pixels (or similar wide ratio)
- **File Size**: Under 500KB (optimize for web)
- **Subject**: Healthcare, medical, doctor, hospital theme
- **Style**: Professional, modern, bright

## Good Image Examples

- Doctor with stethoscope
- Healthcare team smiling
- Modern hospital interior
- Medical technology/equipment
- Patient care scene
- Abstract medical graphics

## Where to Find Free Healthcare Images

- **Unsplash**: https://unsplash.com/s/photos/healthcare
- **Pexels**: https://www.pexels.com/search/medical/
- **Pixabay**: https://pixabay.com/images/search/healthcare/

## Current Setup

The hero section is configured to:
- Show gradient background (default)
- Display your image as a subtle background (when added)
- Maintain text readability with overlay

## After Adding Image

1. Restart the application
2. Refresh your browser (Ctrl + Shift + R)
3. The image should appear in the hero section
